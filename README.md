# My Favorite Movie

## The Hobbit

**The Hobbit** is one of my favorite movies because of its adventure, fantasy, and interesting characters. The story follows Bilbo Baggins as he joins a group of dwarves on an exciting journey to reclaim their homeland.

### Why I Like It

- Exciting adventures
- Great action scenes
- Beautiful locations
- Memorable characters

### My Favorite Character

**Bilbo Baggins** is my favorite character because he starts as a simple and peaceful hobbit but becomes brave and confident throughout his journey.

### Rating

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5
